01 / THE IDEA

Curiosity does not end at the university gate.

Universities offer more than degrees and jobs. They provide seminars, colleagues, feedback and a place to exchange ideas. Outside academia, much of that infrastructure is harder to find.

SCHOLĒ is an attempt to create such a place independently. We are starting in Berlin, with an open invitation to people elsewhere.

02 / WHAT WE WANT TO DO

Research

Discuss ideas, present work in progress and give each other constructive criticism.

Teach

Offer seminars and workshops on subjects we know and care about.

Learn

Read papers together, learn new methods and explore other disciplines.

Open to newcomers. Serious about scientific standards. No PhD required.

03 / GET INVOLVED

Let's start small.

SCHOLĒ is an idea in its early stages, not an established institute. I am looking for people in Berlin who would like to meet, exchange ideas, give a seminar or help build the community.

I'm Johannes, an economist working in the public sector. I started thinking about SCHOLĒ because I want to keep researching and teaching outside a university job.
